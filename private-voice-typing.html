How it works

  1. Tap the bubbleTap any text box. The KisuKisu bubble sits by your keyboard. Tap it.
  2. Talk like you talkUms, restarts, “I mean”. Say it however it comes out.
  3. Tap ✓Clean text lands in the box. Or it's copied for you to paste, if you prefer.

What stays on your phone

Your voice
Turned into text by a speech model on your phone. Never uploaded.
Your text
Tidied by a second AI model on your phone. Never uploaded.
History
Recent dictations, on your phone, so you can copy or fix them. Turn it off or clear it any time.
Your words
Your dictionary, learned words, style examples and tone settings.
Contacts
Optional. Names are read and matched on your phone.

What goes over the internet

Model downloads
The AI models download once from Hugging Face, like any file download. Nothing about you or your dictations is sent.
Cloud AI, only if you choose
You can connect a cloud provider (OpenAI, Groq, Anthropic, OpenRouter and others) with your own API key. Off unless you turn it on.
Everything else
Nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no account server.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no email. Install it and talk.

Is my voice uploaded anywhere?

No. Your audio becomes text on your phone, and a second on-device model tidies it. Neither is sent anywhere. The one exception is a cloud AI provider you choose to connect in Settings with your own API key. That is off unless you turn it on.

Can you read my dictations?

No. We never receive them. There is no server for them to go to.

Does KisuKisu track me?

No. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising IDs, no ads.

Why does it need Accessibility?

To type your words into the box you are in, and to notice when a keyboard is open so the bubble can show. It does not collect or send what is on your screen. Accessibility is optional: without it, your words are copied and you paste them.

Why does it ask for Contacts?

Only if you use “Import from contacts”, to add names to your word list. Names stay on your phone and never uploaded. You can skip it.

Does it keep my recordings?

Only to protect you from losing a take. If a dictation fails, its recording can stay on your phone so you can retry it. You choose how long, or turn it off, in Settings.

How do I delete everything?

Clear history in Settings, or uninstall the app. Everything KisuKisu stored is on your phone and goes with it.
