Requirements, RAM and model sizes

Will it run on my phone?

Short answer: if your phone runs Android 7.0 or newer on a 64-bit chip, yes. Before downloading anything, KisuKisu checks your memory and free space and picks models that fit. A 3 GB phone works.

Questions

What is the minimum Android version?

Android 7.0 (API level 24) or newer.

Does it work on 32-bit phones?

No. KisuKisu needs a 64-bit ARM processor (arm64-v8a). Most Android phones sold in recent years have one.

How much RAM do I need?

It runs on phones with 3 GB. With about 6 GB or more you get the largest, most accurate models. In between, you get a lighter pair.

How much storage does it need?

The app, plus about 316 MB to 1 GB of models depending on your phone, plus about 350 MB of headroom while downloading. If you are short on space, KisuKisu picks smaller models, or tells you how much to free before it starts.

Does it work on Android Go phones?

Yes. On phones Android marks as low on memory, KisuKisu starts with its smallest models.

My phone has 4 GB. Can I use the biggest model anyway?

Yes. Every model is in Settings. The automatic choice is only a starting point. A model that is too big for your phone's memory may be slow or fail, so the app picks a safe one first.

Can I delete models to free space?

Yes. Delete any model you are not using in Settings. You can download it again any time.

Which languages does it understand?

English. Other languages are not supported yet.
