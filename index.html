Questions

Is KisuKisu free?

Yes. KisuKisu is free. No account, no ads, no subscription.

Does KisuKisu work offline?

Yes. Speech recognition and cleanup both run on your phone. After a one-time model download, it works in airplane mode with no signal. More on offline.

Does my voice leave my phone?

No. Your voice is turned into text on your phone and nothing is uploaded. The only exception is if you choose to connect a cloud AI provider in Settings with your own API key. That is off unless you turn it on. More on privacy.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app with a text box: WhatsApp, Gmail, Slack, Notes, your browser. With Accessibility turned on, KisuKisu types the words straight into the box. Without it, they are copied and you paste them. More on apps.

Do I have to switch keyboards?

No. KisuKisu is not a keyboard. It is a small bubble that floats next to the keyboard you already use.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 or newer on a 64-bit (ARM64) phone. It checks your phone's memory and free space and picks models that fit, from about 316 MB on a 3 GB phone to about 1 GB on a 6 GB phone. Full requirements.

Which languages does it understand?

English. Other languages are not supported yet.

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

Android only for now. A Mac version is coming soon. There is no iPhone version.

How do I get KisuKisu?

KisuKisu is in closed testing on Google Play. Fill in the short form with the Google account you use in the Play Store. We add you, usually within a day, and you install it from Google Play.
