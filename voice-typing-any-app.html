Questions

Does KisuKisu replace my keyboard?

No. Keep Gboard, Samsung Keyboard, SwiftKey or whatever you use. KisuKisu is a bubble that floats next to it.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app with a text box. WhatsApp, Gmail, Slack, Telegram, Notes, Google Keep, your browser. If you can type there, you can talk there.

Do I need to turn on Accessibility?

No. Without it, KisuKisu copies your words and you paste them. With it, KisuKisu types them straight into the box, with nothing to paste.

What is the difference between tap and hold?

Tap to start and talk as long as you like, then tap ✓ to send or ✕ to throw it away. Hold for a quick line: talk while you hold, let go to send, or slide onto ✕ to cancel.

Can I move or hide the bubble?

Drag it anywhere above the keyboard. Drop it on the circle to hide it for 10 minutes. Shake the phone to bring it back sooner.

Does it write differently in different apps?

Yes. The AI cleanup uses a tone per kind of app: tidier in email and documents, relaxed in chats. In chat apps it also drops the stiff full stop at the end. You can change the tones in Settings.

Which languages does it understand?

English. Other languages are not supported yet.

How do I get KisuKisu?

KisuKisu is in closed testing on Google Play. Fill in the short form with the Google account you use in the Play Store. We add you, usually within a day, and you install it from Google Play.
